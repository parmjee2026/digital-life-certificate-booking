Required Documents & Details

अपॉइंटमेंट के समय ये दस्तावेज़ और जानकारी अपने पास रखें।

  • Aadhaar Card / Aadhaar number or VID — Aadhaar details must be available for authentication.
  • Mobile phone — keep the mobile number active to receive OTP / SMS.
  • PPO Number — Pension Payment Order details.
  • Pension bank passbook — keep pension account number and bank/branch details handy.
  • Pension information — pension type, Pension Sanctioning Authority and Pension Disbursing Agency details, if known.
  • Biometric authentication — fingerprint or iris authentication may be required, depending on the service method.

Privacy: Please bring/show these details during the appointment. Do not send Aadhaar, PPO, bank details, OTP, or document photos through this website or WhatsApp unless you have independently verified a secure and necessary process.
